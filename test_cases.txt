STUDENT REGISTRATION SYSTEM - TEST CASES

1. Valid record:
   Name: Aarav Sharma
   Phone: 9876543210
   Email: aarav.sharma@gmail.com
   Expected: Accepted

2. Invalid phone length:
   Phone: 12345
   Expected: Rejected - phone must contain exactly 10 digits

3. Invalid phone prefix:
   Phone: 5551234567
   Expected: Rejected - phone must start with 6, 7, 8, or 9

4. Missing phone:
   Phone: blank
   Expected: Rejected - phone must contain exactly 10 digits

5. Missing name:
   Name: blank
   Expected: Rejected - name is blank

6. Invalid email with two @ symbols:
   Email: student@@gmail.com
   Expected: Rejected - email must contain exactly one @ symbol

7. Invalid email domain:
   Email: student@gmail
   Expected: Rejected - email domain must contain a valid period

8. Duplicate phone:
   Use a phone number already in the student list.
   Expected: Rejected - duplicate phone number

9. Duplicate email:
   Use an email already in the student list.
   Expected: Rejected - duplicate email address

10. Search:
    Search by a student's name, phone number, and email.
    Expected: Matching record is displayed.

11. Update:
    Enter a student ID and change one or more fields.
    Expected: Updated record is saved to students_clean.csv.

12. Delete:
    Enter a student ID or phone number and confirm with y.
    Expected: Record is removed and clean CSV is updated.

13. Analytics:
    Select menu option 6.
    Expected: Acceptance rate, rejection reasons, phone prefixes, and email domains are displayed.
